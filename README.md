# CustomerAnalysis_Excel
Excel-based data analysis project using KPMG customer data. Covers data cleaning, segmentation, transaction trends, CLV modeling, and strategic recommendations—all built in Excel with dashboards and insights.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

The objective of this project is to assist a bicycle company in understanding its customer base, optimizing sales performance, and identifying growth opportunities through data-driven strategies.

The company provided:
- Customer demographic, address, and transaction data
- A list of newly acquired customers
- A business brief with performance expectations

---

## 📊 Task Overview

### 🔹 Task 1 – Data Cleaning
- Removed null or inconsistent values across address, demographics, and transactions
- Standardized columns (gender, job industry, tenure, dates)
- Used Excel formulas, Power Query transformations, and conditional logic

### 🔹 Task 2 – Customer Segmentation
- Grouped customers by wealth segment, tenure, gender, and job industry
- Visualized customer distribution using PivotTables and charts
- Identified key segments: Mass customers, male purchasers, and finance/manufacturing workers

### 🔹 Task 3 – Transaction Analysis
- Analyzed monthly sales trends, brand performance, and product line revenue
- Found October as peak month and Solex as top-selling brand
- Suggested seasonal promotions and product stocking strategies

### 🔹 Task 4 – New Customer Insights
- Segmented new customer base by region, industry, and wealth category
- Found NSW and the Mass segment to be dominant
- Provided recommendations for targeted marketing and onboarding

### 🔹 Task 5 – Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Analysis
- Estimated CLV by customer type, gender, and industry
- Highlighted top 10 customers contributing 18% of future revenue
- Suggested retention strategies and CLV-driven dashboards

### 🔹 Task 6 – Summary & Recommendation
- Delivered executive insights across all tasks
- Strategic recommendations for marketing, product management, and customer retention
- Provided a link to a video walkthrough of the final report
